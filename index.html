Telugu Purana RAG

Grounded Telugu answers over 17 Puranas that exist only in Sanskrit. A generated Telugu chapter-card layer makes the search same-language; the Sanskrit verses go to the model; every quoted sloka is verified word for word and the meaning is translated on demand.

2,809chapters carded, 141k Sanskrit verses
0.90recall@10 on 96 Telugu questions
0.945faithfulness, LLM judge (5 answers)
