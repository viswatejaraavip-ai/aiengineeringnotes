Start with what you would do by hand

Five houses in a row hold 2, 7, 9, 3 and 1 rupees. A thief may rob any houses but never two neighbours, because the alarms are linked. What is the most the thief can take? By hand you try combinations: 2 + 9 + 1 = 12, 7 + 3 = 10, 2 + 3 = 5, 7 + 1 = 8, 9 alone... The best is 12.

Trying combinations means, for each house, "rob or not", and 25 = 32 choices here. For 100 houses that is 2100, more than the number of grains of sand on Earth many times over. We need a way to reuse work instead of re-deciding every house for every combination.

Build the structure, one piece at a time

Piece one: a question with a small answer. Let dp[i] mean "the most you can take from houses 0 to i only". The final answer is dp[4].

Piece two: notice that the decision for house i has only two cases. Skip it: then the best is whatever the best was for houses 0 to i − 1, which is dp[i − 1]. Rob it: then house i − 1 is off limits and the best is nums[i] plus the best for houses 0 to i − 2, which is dp[i − 2]. Take the larger:

dp[i] = max( dp[i − 1], dp[i − 2] + nums[i] )

Piece four: the table only ever looks back two places, so you do not need the whole array. Keep two variables, "best so far" and "best before that", and roll them forward.

Why it is correct

The invariant: after computing dp[i], it is the true best for the prefix ending at i. The base cases are right: dp[0] = nums[0], dp[1] = max of the two. For a larger i, any legal plan for houses 0 .. i either robs house i or not, and those two cases are exactly the two terms in the formula, each resting on a smaller prefix whose value is already correct. So the maximum of the two is the true best.

Why it is fast

Each entry costs one comparison and one addition, and there are n entries:

work = n steps, memory = 2 numbers

For 100 houses that is 100 steps instead of 2100 combinations. The saving comes entirely from the fact that the best for a prefix does not depend on how it was achieved, only on its value.

How to recognise it in an interview

Cues: "cannot pick two adjacent", "ways to reach step n with steps of 1 or 2", "minimum cost to climb", anything where the answer for position i depends on positions i − 1 and i − 2 only. The first thing to say: "define dp[i] for the prefix, write the two-case recurrence, and roll two variables." House Robber is the example above. Climbing Stairs is the same shape with addition instead of max: ways[i] = ways[i − 1] + ways[i − 2], the Fibonacci numbers.
